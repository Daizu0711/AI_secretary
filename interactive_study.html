📐 Key Formulas

  • Wavelength: λ = 2L/n (fixed-fixed/open-open), λ = 4L/n (n odd, closed-open)
  • Frequency: f = v/λ = n·v/(2L) or n·v/(4L)
  • Nodes: Fixed end = Node, Open end = Antinode
  • Harmonics: Only odd harmonics for closed-open pipes
